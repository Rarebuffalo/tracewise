# Code of Conduct

Tracewise adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
as its code of conduct. The full text at that link applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions and reviews) and to anyone representing the project.

In short, we expect participants to:

- be respectful and welcoming, including towards differing viewpoints and experience levels;
- give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its users;
- take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks,
publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered
inappropriate.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainer, [@Rarebuffalo](https://github.com/Rarebuffalo),
through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject
contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do not follow this code, as described in the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
