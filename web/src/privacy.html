Waitlist privacy notice

Draft, version {{CONSENT_VERSION}}. This notice describes how the waitlist on this site works as built. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer yet and will be reviewed before any pilot. It covers only this website and its waitlist, not the Tracewise GitHub App (which is not available yet).

Who runs this

The waitlist is run by {{OPERATOR}}. Questions and requests: {{CONTACT}}.

What we collect when you join

  • Your email address (required), to confirm it's yours and to invite you when a pilot slot opens.
  • Your GitHub username, only if you give it, to help plan invites. We don't use it to access anything.
  • Whether you ticked "product updates", with the time and the version of the wording you saw. The box is unticked unless you tick it.
  • Your answer to "how often does CI fail on you?", only if you answer.
  • When you signed up and when you confirmed.
  • Whether your email domain is a known disposable-mail service, to spot abuse. This is a flag, not a block.

What we don't collect

  • No analytics or advertising trackers on this site, and this site sets no cookies.
  • No open or click tracking in our emails.
  • Your IP address is used briefly to limit repeated requests. We store only a keyed hash of it in short counters that are deleted within two days.
  • Confirmation and removal links carry their codes after a #, which browsers don't send to servers, so they don't end up in request logs.

Who processes it

  • Hosting: Vercel serves this site and the signup API. Like any host, it processes requests (including IP addresses) to deliver them and may keep its own request logs.
  • Database: {{DATABASE_PROVIDER}} stores the waitlist, separately from any other Tracewise data.
  • Email: Resend delivers the confirmation email and any later early-access emails.
  • Bot protection: Cloudflare Turnstile checks that the signup form is used by a person. To do that, Cloudflare processes your IP address and signals from your browser.

When you confirm, the operator receives one notification email with your signup details (the fields above). We don't sell or share the list with anyone else.

How long we keep it

  • Signups that are never confirmed are deleted after 7 days. Confirmation links expire after {{PUBLIC_VERIFY_TTL_HOURS}} hours.
  • Confirmed signups are kept until you remove yourself or ask us to delete them. We'll revisit this before launch.
  • Deleting your signup also deletes its email-delivery records. The operator's copy of the notification email isn't deleted automatically; we'll delete it on request.

Your choices

  • Leave the list: every email has a "remove me" link, which deletes your signup straight away.
  • See, correct, export or delete your data: contact {{CONTACT}} from the address you signed up with.
  • Product updates: if you ticked the box and change your mind, use the remove link or contact us.

Changes

If this notice changes, the version above changes with it.
